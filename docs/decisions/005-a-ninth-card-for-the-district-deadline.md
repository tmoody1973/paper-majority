# 005 — The spike desk carries nine card types, not eight

- **Decision** — The interaction spike ships nine card types on the desk: the eight the rules need, plus an Urgent Renter Concern that belongs to no rule and exists only to carry a visible deadline.

- **Why this came up** — The plan asks for exactly eight card types, then separately asks the fixture to "also contain one expiring district concern". Those cannot both be true. The three rules plus the plan's named requirements consume all eight slots exactly: one policy staffer, two housing reports, one renter policy, the bill, two member offices, one Tactic. Nothing is left over.

  What is at stake: one of the six playtest thresholds. We need at least 8 of 10 testers to blame a setback on a card, a deadline or an unfinished job rather than on a number going down. That test needs something on the desk with a deadline on it.

- **Options**
  1. **Ship eight and drop the deadline.** Hits the stated number. Removes the only cue that makes one of the six thresholds measurable.
  2. **Ship eight and put the deadline on a Rent Burden Report.** Hits the number, but a research report expiring is not a district obligation. It would teach testers the wrong thing about what causes a setback, and we would be measuring the wrong instinct.
  3. **Ship nine (chosen).** Misses the stated count by one; keeps the mechanic and the meaning intact.

- **What we chose and why** — Option 3, by Claude, flagged for Tarik's decision at Checkpoint 1. The extra card takes part in no rule, so the pattern-density gate still reports exactly four valid combinations before the Tactic and five after — the number that actually protects the design is unaffected.

- **What we gave up** — A stated gate number is not met exactly, and that has to be declared rather than buried. There is also one more card on an already busy desk, which slightly raises the reading load in a test that is partly about reading load.

- **How we will know if this was right** — Two checks. Does anyone in the playtest name the renter concern as the cause of a setback? And does the extra card make the desk feel crowded in the free-notes section? If it earns neither its keep nor a complaint, drop it and accept losing that threshold's cue.
