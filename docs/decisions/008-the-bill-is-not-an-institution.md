# 008 — The Working Bill moves from the Institution family to Policy

- **Decision** — The player's own bill is a **Policy** card, not an **Institution**
  card. Its identifier changes from `institution-working-bill` to `policy-working-bill`.

- **Why this came up** — The eight card families each answer "what does this card do".
  *Institution* means the machinery Congress works through — committees, the floor, the
  calendar; things that control procedural access.

  A bill is not machinery. It is the thing being pushed *through* the machinery. Filing
  it under Institution taught players something false about how Congress works, in a
  game whose stated purpose is to make people curious about how Congress works.

  It surfaced when the plain-English key went on screen. Once the key said "Institution
  — the machinery Congress works through", the mismatch was impossible to miss. Making
  the vocabulary legible made a taxonomy error legible too.

  What is at stake: the family taxonomy freezes when the 30-card catalog is authored.
  A wrong family is cheap now and expensive after that.

- **Options**
  1. **Leave it under Institution.** No work. Teaches a falsehood and locks it in.
  2. **Move it to Policy (chosen).** Of the eight approved families, Policy is the one
     that means "the legislative substance you are building". The bill is the
     accumulated substance.
  3. **Give the bill its own family.** Honest, but it breaks the approved eight-family
     taxonomy, the eight icons in the illustration kit and the family colour system.
  4. **Make it a desk zone rather than a card.** Almost certainly the real answer — the
     design already lists a **Bill Docket** anchor that "holds the current
     signature-bill version". But zones are Task 7 work, and the interaction spike
     needs the bill to be draggable today.

- **What we chose and why** — Option 2, by Tarik. It is correct enough to stop teaching
  a falsehood, costs one field and one pattern slot, and does not touch the approved
  taxonomy. The pattern-density gate still reports exactly four valid combinations
  before the Tactic and five after.

- **What we gave up** — Policy is not a perfect home either. A bill is a container for
  provisions, not a provision itself, so the family now holds two different kinds of
  thing. Option 4 remains the right long-term answer and is deferred, not dismissed.

- **How we will know if this was right** — Two checks. When the Bill Docket zone is
  built in Task 7, does the bill stop being a card entirely? And in the playtest, does
  anyone describe the bill as a piece of Congress rather than as their own work?
