# 010 — The Drafted Provision tells the player it is the end of the chain

- **Decision** — Leave the rules alone. Make the card say what it is: the last step of
  the chain in this practice run. Its subtitle reads **"Bill language — end of the
  chain"**, and hovering it explains that the full game adds a step for putting it into
  your bill.

- **Why this came up** — Tarik completed the two-step chain, got a card labelled "Bill
  language", and asked where it goes. The answer is nowhere: it fits no slot in any of
  the three rules. Dragging it onto the Working Bill — now the same card family — bounces.

  What is at stake: this happens at the most satisfying moment in the ten minutes. If
  the game's reply to a player's correct instinct is a silent bounce, it reads as
  broken, and it costs us the "want another go" measure.

- **Options**
  1. **Say so, change nothing (chosen).** The card explains its own limit. Rules
     untouched, gate stays at exactly 4 and 5 valid stacks.
  2. **Let it into the bill.** A fourth rule. Breaks "exactly three learnable patterns",
     moves the stack counts, and forces the density gate, the guide and the worksheet to
     be redone.

- **What we chose and why** — Option 1, by Tarik. The gate is asking whether learning
  the rules is satisfying. Changing the number of rules during the test means no longer
  testing the thing we set out to test. The dead end is in the approved plan, which
  specifies exactly three patterns; what was missing was the game admitting it.

- **What we gave up** — The chain still stops short of the thing it is obviously for.
  Explaining a limit is weaker than removing it, and a tester may still find it
  unsatisfying — they will just know it is deliberate rather than broken.

- **How we will know if this was right** — The playtest guide now tells facilitators to
  note whether testers seem annoyed when the Drafted Provision bounces off the bill. If
  that shows up repeatedly in the free notes, "add a provision to your bill" becomes the
  first thing Phase 2 builds.
