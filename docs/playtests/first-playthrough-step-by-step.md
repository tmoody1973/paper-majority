# Paper Majority: your first playthrough test

**Test status: not played yet.** Mark your results and fill in the blanks as you go. This guide does not record a test as passed for you.

You do not need to know coding or politics. Allow about 30–45 minutes to play, read, and take notes. You can stop whenever you want.

## What are you trying to do?

You run a small congressional office. Your team has six in-game weeks to prepare a housing bill and get other offices behind it.

Your goal is:

- Two **different provisions** in your bill. A provision is one part of a proposed law.
- Two **other offices committed** to supporting it.
- No missed **mandatory commitments**. Mandatory means required.

You are getting a proposal ready for its next step. You are not passing a law in this session.

You are also testing the game. Getting confused is useful feedback. Losing does not automatically mean the game is broken.

## Before you start

Open [Paper Majority](http://127.0.0.1:3100/) on the computer running the game. This is a local address, not a public website. If it will not open, ask the person running the test to start the game.

Use a separate browser profile for this test if there is a saved game you want to keep. Otherwise, use **Export current checkpoint** on the setup screen before starting over. Keep that downloaded backup. Starting a new Session changes the current run.

## Step 3 — Find your controls and pause

1. Find the button at the top labeled **Pause** or **Resume**.
2. If it says **Pause**, click it. If it says **Resume**, you are already paused.
3. Find **Work Mat**, **Bill Docket**, and **Deadlines**. Scroll if needed.

The Work Mat starts jobs. The Bill Docket holds the parts of your bill. Deadlines tell you what needs attention and when.

**You should see:** the normal timer stays still while paused. You can still inspect cards and prepare jobs. **Fast-forward to next event** deliberately advances time even when you are paused.

## Step 4 — Try a combination that does not work

Stay paused. In **Work Mat**:

1. Use **Desk card** to choose **Housing Choice Voucher Support**.

3. Choose **Rent Burden Report** and click **Stage card** again.
4. Read the message. Do not add a staff member yet.
5. Use **Remove** to take both cards out of the Work Mat.

**You should see:** no valid job, a disabled **Begin Work** button, and no loss of resources or cards. Staging cards only previews a job.

- Result (choose one): **Worked / Confusing / Broken**

## Step 5 — Make your first evidence summary

Use this method whenever this guide says to run a job:

1. Choose one card under **Desk card**, then click **Stage card**.
2. Repeat for every card in the recipe.
3. Read the **Ready:** preview: result, time, cost, **Consumes**, and **Returns**.
4. Click **Begin Work** only when the preview matches the job you want.
5. Click **Fast-forward to next event**. If a choice interrupts, answer it first. If the job is still working, fast-forward again after checking deadlines.
6. Check the finished result. The staff member should become available again.

Your first recipe is:

**Policy Aide + Rent Burden Report → Rent Burden Report — summary**

The summary is a prepared version of that report. **Keep it for the renter request in Week 2.** Do not spend it on a draft yet. Evidence used for one job cannot also be used for another.

**You should see:** the summary in the card list and the staff member returned. The Week 1 agenda can still say the bill needs a provision; this guide intentionally saves the report first.

## Step 6 — Move to Week 2 and keep your first promise

1. After your current work finishes, use **Fast-forward to next event** until the Week 1 review appears. Answer any pending choices along the way.
2. Read the review, then click **Apply review and start week 2**.
3. Answer the new Story choice.
4. Click the button beginning **Open Week 2:** to receive that week's cards.
5. Open **All commitments and deadlines**. Find **Earn a renter-priority endorsement** and write down its deadline.
6. Run this job before that deadline:

**An available staff member + Urgent Renter Concern + Rent Burden Report — summary**

Use the full three-card job. A staff member plus the concern alone is only a basic response; it does not earn the required endorsement.

**You should see:** the endorsement job finishes, the report is spent, and the commitment becomes **fulfilled**. Look for a prepared version of the concern. Completing the promise does not by itself mean another office supports your bill.

## Step 8 — Draft and add your first provision in Week 3

1. Finish Week 2 using the review process from Step 6.
2. In Week 3, answer the Story and click **Open Week 3:**.
3. Find the new report: **Housing Needs Tables** or **Fair Market Rent Benchmarks**.
4. Run **an available staff member + the new raw report** to make a summary.
5. Run **an available staff member + Housing Choice Voucher Support + that summary**.
6. When the draft finishes, find **Bill Docket**.
7. Under **Drafted provision**, choose **Housing Choice Voucher Support**. Read its preview, then click **Add to Bill**.

**You should see:** the provision listed in your bill, its explanation, and a new revision number. Drafting creates a card; **Add to Bill** puts it into the actual proposal.

## Step 9 — Ask an office for support

Try this job once your first provision is in the bill:

1. Start only if the preview says **Ready: Office decision**.
2. Finish the work and read the offer.
3. Read the cost and conditions before choosing. Accept an affordable offer if you can meet its conditions, or refuse it and note why.

At the start of each week: answer the Story, open the **Open Week N:** pack, and read your deadlines. After each job, check the result before starting another.

## If you get stuck

| What happened | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Begin Work is disabled | Check that all cards are idle, on the desk, and in the right form: raw, summary, prepared, or drafted. Read the rejection message. |
| Work is not moving | You may be paused. Use Resume or deliberately fast-forward after reading deadlines. |
| Fast-forward is disabled | Answer the pending Story or office decision. Closing its window does not answer it. |
| A needed card is missing | It may be working, spent, filed, or not supplied yet. Do not restart without recording what is missing. |
| An office will not accept another approach | Finish or cancel its active outreach. It can also have already answered the current version of your bill. |
| A final-week job cannot start | Read whether it has enough time to finish before the Session ends. |
| You missed a required deadline | Record the reason. Continue to test the ending; do not assume the missed commitment can be undone. |

If the instructions still do not help, stop and write the exact button, card, and message involved. Do not keep clicking random buttons to make the problem disappear.
