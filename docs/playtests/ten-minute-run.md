# The Ten-Minute Run, step by step

**Who this is for:** the person checking the game works, or doing their own run.

> ⚠️ **Never show this page to a tester before they play.** The whole test is
> whether a stranger can work the rules out alone. This page gives every answer
> away. Testers get one sentence: *"You run a congressional office. Drag cards
> together."* That's it.

**Time:** about ten minutes. The game itself only needs 44 seconds of waiting.
The rest is you looking and thinking.

**Every number on this page was checked against the real game on 2026-09-01.**
If your screen shows a different number, that's a bug. Write it down.

---

## Before you start

1. Open this link in Chrome or Safari:

   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:3001/?fixture=interaction-spike
   ```

2. Press **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Windows). This resets the game.

3. Look at the screen. You should see:
   - A strip across the top with the word **Democratic** and six numbers
   - **12 cards** on a beige desk, in three rows
   - A panel on the right that starts with **Your office**

4. Write down the six numbers at the top. They should be:

   | Staff Attention | Political Capital | District Trust | Bill Momentum | Policy Integrity | Staff Morale |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | **3** | **3** | **60** | **10** | **60** | **70** |

5. Click the **Resume** button at the top right. It changes to **Pause**. Leave it.
   (The game starts frozen. Nothing moves until you press this.)

**What the cards are, in one line each:**

- **Policy Aide** (two of them) — a person who works for you. They do the jobs.
- **Rent Burden Report** and **Tenant Survey** (two of each) — facts about housing.
- **Housing Choice Voucher** (two) — an idea that could go into your law.
- **Working Bill** — the law you're writing.
- **Hillcrest Member Office** and **Ridgeline Member Office** — two other politicians. Hillcrest is on your team (D). Ridgeline is on the other team (R).
- **Bipartisan Working Group** — a new trick your team can learn.

---

## The run

Each step: **drag one card and drop it on top of another.** Then watch.

### Step 1 — Turn a report into a summary *(6 seconds)*

**Drag** a **Policy Aide** onto a **Rent Burden Report**.

**You'll see:** the two cards snap into a pile. A green bar fills up along the
bottom. After about six seconds the pile splits apart:

- The report is **gone**. In its place is a new card, **Evidence Summary**, with
  small blue words at the bottom: *From: Rent Burden Report*.
- The Policy Aide is **back** on the desk, ready for more work.
- The line under the top strip says: *"Your aide turned the official report into a
  summary a committee will take seriously. **Policy Aide is free again.**"*
- **Bill Momentum** goes from 10 to **13**.

**The rule you just learned:** people do the work and come back. Paper gets used up.

### Step 2 — Do it again, with the other kind of facts *(6 seconds)*

**Drag** a **Policy Aide** onto a **Tenant Survey**.

**You'll see:** the same thing happens, but:

- The new summary says *From: Tenant Survey*. Now you have two summaries and they
  look different at the bottom.
- The line says *"...a summary that **speaks for the district**..."*
- **District Trust** goes from 60 to **63**. (The report helped your bill. The
  survey helped your reputation back home. Different facts, different results.)

**Also notice:** the Policy Aide cards and the fact cards now say *"Ties up 1
staffer"* at the bottom left. They didn't before. The game only tells you what
something costs once you've seen the rule work.

### Step 3 — Turn a summary into law words *(6 seconds)*

**Drag** an **Evidence Summary** onto a **Housing Choice Voucher**.

**You'll see:** after six seconds, both cards are gone and a **Drafted Provision**
appears. That's a piece of your law, written out.

- The line says: *"That summary is now bill language."*
- **Bill Momentum** goes from 13 to **15**.

**Now try this:** drag the **Drafted Provision** onto the **Working Bill**.

**You'll see:** it bounces off. The line says *"Those two do not go together.
Nothing was spent."* That's correct. Putting the provision **into** the bill is a
later part of the game that isn't built yet. Nothing bad happened. Nothing was lost.

### Step 4 — Ask a politician for help *(6 seconds)*

**Drag** the **Working Bill** onto **Hillcrest Member Office**.

**You'll see:** after six seconds, a new card, **Outreach Result**, with *From:
Hillcrest Member Office* at the bottom. **Both the Working Bill and Hillcrest are
still on the desk.** Asking someone for help doesn't use them up.

- The line says: *"The office is on board, in this simulation. **Hillcrest Member
  Office and Working Bill are free again.**"*
- **Political Capital** goes from 3 to **2**. That's the one thing that does NOT
  come back. You spent a favor.
- **Bill Momentum** goes from 15 to **17**.
- Hillcrest's card now says *"Spends 1 political capital"* at the bottom.

### Step 5 — Ask the OTHER team's politician *(instant)*

**Drag** the **Working Bill** onto **Ridgeline Member Office**.

**You'll see:** it bounces off. But read the line:

> *"Not yet. Studying Bipartisan Working Group would let this rule accept a card
> from the other party. Nothing was spent."*

The game just told you exactly what to do next. Nothing changed. All six numbers
are the same as before.

### Step 6 — Learn the trick *(8 seconds)*

**Drag** a **Policy Aide** onto **Bipartisan Working Group**.

**You'll see:** the line says *"Your aide is studying the Tactic."* A green bar
fills up for about eight seconds. Then:

- The Bipartisan Working Group card is **gone** — your team learned it.
- The Policy Aide is **back**.
- The line says: *"Rule changed. Check the Staff Handbook."*
- The numbers don't change.

**Now click "Staff Handbook"** (top right). Look at the top of the panel:

- *3 of 3 rules found*
- *1 of 1 Tactics learned*
- A box that says the outreach rule now works with: **shares your party, from the
  other party**

Click **Close Handbook**.

### Step 7 — Try Ridgeline again *(6 seconds)*

**Drag** the **Working Bill** onto **Ridgeline Member Office**. The same thing that
bounced in Step 5.

**You'll see:** it works this time. After six seconds, a second **Outreach Result**,
*From: Ridgeline Member Office*.

- The line says: *"The office will help — in this simulation — **if you take their
  amendment.**"* That means: they'll help, but they want you to change your law a
  little.
- **Political Capital** goes from 2 to **1**.
- **Bill Momentum** goes from 17 to **18**.
- **Policy Integrity** goes from 60 to **59**. That's the price. You gave up a
  little of what you promised, to get the other team on board.

**That was the whole point of the run.** A rule was too narrow. You learned a
trick. The rule got wider. Something that used to bounce now works.

### Step 8 — Try something silly *(instant)*

**Drag** a **Housing Choice Voucher** onto **Hillcrest Member Office**.

**You'll see:** it bounces. *"Those two do not go together. Nothing was spent."*
Check the numbers. Nothing moved. Trying things is always free.

## Two extra things worth trying

**Pull someone out of a job.** Start Step 1 again with the other Rent Burden
Report, and while the green bar is filling, drag the Policy Aide off the pile onto
empty desk. The job stops. The aide is free. Staff Attention is still 3. Nothing
was lost except the time.

**Ask Hillcrest twice.** Drag the Working Bill onto Hillcrest again. It works
again and makes another Outreach Result. That's a known quirk we're watching. Does
it feel like cheating, or like a bug? Write down which.

## Words on the cards you might wonder about

| Words | What they mean |
|---|---|
| **Official record** | Real public facts from a real source |
| **Based on records** | Worked out from real facts. A summary, not a quote |
| **Simulated** | Made up for this game. Not about any real person |
| **Practice card — no source** | This is a practice game. This card stands in for a real document, but we haven't attached the real source yet |
| **From: ...** | You made this card. It came from the card named |
| **Ties up 1 staffer** | Using this keeps one of your people busy. You get them back |
| **Spends 1 political capital** | Using this costs a favor. You don't get it back |

Hillcrest and Ridgeline are not real people. The district is made up too.
