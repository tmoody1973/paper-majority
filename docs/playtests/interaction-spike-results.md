## What this test is for

The spike is a ten-minute slice with eight starting card definitions, three learnable
rules and one Tactic that widens one of them. It exists to answer one question before
any content is built at scale:

> Does family/tag experimentation, discovery and the Tactic rule change feel good and
> read clearly enough to justify building the 30-definition six-week scenario?

## How to run one session

1. Start the game and open the printed URL. Do not reuse a stale `localhost` link.
2. Say one sentence only: **"You run a congressional office. Drag cards together."**
3. Demonstrate exactly one combination: drag the Policy Aide onto the Rent Burden Report.
4. Say nothing else. Do not point at the Staff Handbook. Do not explain the Tactic.
5. Let the tester play for up to ten minutes.
6. Ask the questions below afterwards, in this order.
7. Record answers verbatim where the field asks for words.

Do not coach. A tester who is stuck is data.

## Thresholds this test must clear

| # | Measure | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Predicts three valid stacks after one demonstration | at least 8 of 10 |
| 2 | Discovers two rules without opening the Handbook | at least 7 of 10 |
| 3 | Can explain what the Tactic changed | at least 8 of 10 |
| 4 | Identifies card family and information class without the inspector | at least 9 of 10 |
| 5 | Chooses to start another run | at least 7 of 10 |
| 6 | Names the card, deadline or unfinished obligation behind a setback — not just a meter | at least 8 of 10 |
