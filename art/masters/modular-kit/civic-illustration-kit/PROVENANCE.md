# Asset Provenance

The Paper Majority Civic Illustration Kit is project-authored vector artwork generated from deterministic Python source. It was revised from the supplied Civic Illustration Kit concept to align with the approved Paper Majority card-family, provenance, localization, accessibility and representation rules.

No IconScout, Unicons, stock-art, campaign, news-service or real-lawmaker image files are embedded in this kit.

Third-party icons may be used separately for ordinary interface controls only after their license and attribution requirements are recorded. They must not be copied into this kit or represented as project-authored artwork without an applicable license record.
